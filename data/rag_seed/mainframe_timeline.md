# BigIron Mainframe Timeline

These are safe, original timeline notes for local RAG and deterministic direct-answer extraction. They are not copied from IBM manuals or Redbooks.

### System/360
Aliases: system/360; system 360; s/360; s 360; ibm 360
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**IBM System/360**
- Announced on April 7, 1964.
- Mainframe angle: System/360 established the compatible architecture family that System/370 later extended.
- If the user asks when it came out, answer with the date first, not a generic mainframe overview.

### System/370
Aliases: system/370; system 370; s/370; s 370; ibm 370; 370 architecture
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**IBM System/370**
- Announced on June 30, 1970 as the successor to System/360.
- First customer shipments for early models followed in 1971.
- Mainframe angle: System/370 is the hardware architecture family; MVS arrived later in the operating-system lineage, and z/OS is much later.
- If the user asks when it came out, the direct answer is **June 30, 1970**.

### MVS
Aliases: mvs; multiple virtual storage
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**MVS**
- MVS, Multiple Virtual Storage, dates to 1974.
- Later lineage includes MVS/XA, MVS/ESA, OS/390, and z/OS.
- Mainframe angle: MVS is an operating-system lineage, not the same thing as System/370 hardware.

### MVS/ESA
Aliases: mvs/esa; mvs esa; mvs sp version 3; mvs/sp version 3
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**MVS/ESA**
- Announced as MVS/SP Version 3 on February 15, 1988.
- It brought the MVS line into the Enterprise Systems Architecture era before OS/390.

### System/390
Aliases: system/390; system 390; s/390; s 390; esa/390; mvs 390; mvs/390
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**IBM System/390**
- IBM System/390 and ESA/390 were announced on September 5, 1990.
- MVS/ESA was announced earlier, on February 15, 1988.
- OS/390 followed in late 1995 as the packaged successor in the MVS line.
- If the user says `MVS 390`, clarify whether they mean MVS/ESA or the System/390 generation.

### OS/390
Aliases: os/390; os 390
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**OS/390**
- Introduced in late 1995 as IBM's packaged successor in the MVS family for System/390.
- Mainframe angle: OS/390 sits between MVS/ESA and z/OS in the operating-system lineage.

### z/OS
Aliases: z/os; z os; zos
Question intent: date, announced, released, introduced, came out, come out, coming out
Answer:
**z/OS**
- z/OS Version 1 Release 1 became generally available in 2001 as the 64-bit successor in the OS/390 line.
- Mainframe angle: z/OS is not System/370 hardware; it belongs to the later z/Architecture era.
